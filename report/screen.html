Survivorship. Historical discount and return averages are computed from funds that still exist. Vehicles wound up or taken over during the window are absent from those averages, which flatters both — most closed-end funds die at a discount or via a premium-closing corporate action, so the bias runs in both directions and is not estimated here.
NTA basis differs by market. ASX LICs are carried on pre-tax NTA (the figure the ASX report leads with); UK trusts on cum-income NAV. Post-tax NTA is stored but never blended into the same series. Cross-market discount comparisons inherit this inconsistency — an Australian LIC with large deferred tax looks cheaper on pre-tax NTA than a UK trust with the same economics.
